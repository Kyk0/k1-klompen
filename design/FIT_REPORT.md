## Thickness (measured on the exported STL)

- **Arch sole:** 10.0 mm minimum (7 mm-deep arch relief).
- **Sole above pad pockets:** 13 mm.
- **Above screw heads:** 4 mm.
- **Upper shell:** 2.4 mm nominal. Under the 0.7 mm carving grooves, 1.7 mm remains.
- **Not raised to 3 mm:** the rim lip and the throat edge still taper to a thin edge where the clearance cuts for full ankle roll and dorsiflexion meet the outer surface. A 3 mm minimum there added a visible ledge at the heel rim, so it was dropped in favour of the v5 look and shorter print time.

Pad borders: 5.013 mm (front), 5.370 mm (heel). STL hashes and per-pose results are in `validation.json`.
